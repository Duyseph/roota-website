About ROOTA

Making Commuting Easier to Navigate.

ROOTA is a commuter-focused mobile application designed to make transportation information more accessible and convenient.

We aim to help Filipino commuters make more informed travel decisions by bringing useful transportation information and commuter resources together in one place.

  • Access helpful commuter information
  • Learn about routes and transportation options
  • Understand fares and applicable discounts
  • Explore useful commuter guides and resources
